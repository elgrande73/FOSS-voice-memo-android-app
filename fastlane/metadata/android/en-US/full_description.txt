<p><b>Voice Memo</b> records voice memos hands-free. Say "Mango Tango" to start a memo and say it again to stop. Memos can be turned into text on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Wake phrase "Mango Tango", detected on the phone. For reliable detection, record the phrase (or any other phrase) in your own voice; up to 5 samples.</li>
<li>Or let any sound above a level you choose start a memo (Loudness Only mode).</li>
<li>One-tap recording: a large Quick Memo button, a Quick Settings tile, a home-screen widget, a "Record memo" launcher shortcut, a button in the monitoring notification and an optional Bluetooth/USB keyboard shortcut.</li>
<li>A short buffer keeps the first word; memos end after a pause you choose, when you repeat the wake phrase, or when you tap Stop.</li>
<li>Speech-to-text on the phone with whisper.cpp and the Whisper base model: English by default, 99 languages, or automatic detection. Edit or delete transcripts; optional automatic transcription.</li>
<li>History with search, filters, favourites, playback speed and autoplay through the list.</li>
<li>Export one memo as WAV or many as a ZIP with the WAV files and a CSV/JSON list.</li>
<li>Built-in, USB and Bluetooth headset microphones; live level meter in dBFS.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Network use</b></p>
<p>The app does not include a speech model. Transcription needs the OpenAI Whisper "base" model (MIT license, about 148 MB), which the app downloads from the whisper.cpp project's files on Hugging Face only when you tap <i>Download Whisper Base model</i> in Settings. The file is verified against a published checksum. This is the app's only internet use; recording, wake-phrase detection and transcription work offline. You can also import a whisper.cpp model file instead.</p>
<p><b>Good to know</b></p>
<ul>
<li>The built-in wake phrase was made from synthetic voices and does not recognise every voice; recording it in your own voice is recommended.</li>
<li>While monitoring runs, memos can be started and stopped from the lock screen (notification button and tile). You can turn this off in Settings.</li>
<li>Transcription speed depends on the phone; long memos can take a while on older devices.</li>
</ul>
<p>Voice Memo replaces an earlier app with a different name and application ID. It is installed as a separate app and cannot access the earlier app's data.</p>
