<p><b>Voice Memo</b> records voice memos automatically and hands-free. All detection runs on your device: no accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no ads, and no internet permission.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Automatic recording when speech or sound exceeds a configurable activation threshold; stops after a configurable silence timeout</li>
<li>Wake phrase "Ciao Linda" to start (and optionally stop) a memo, with an offline keyword spotter; record the phrase in your own voice for a personal template</li>
<li>One <i>Match confidence threshold</i> (20–80 %) with a live match gauge on the dashboard</li>
<li>Start and stop memos from the lock screen (notification button and Quick Settings tile), with a large record button on the dashboard</li>
<li>Optional shortcuts: hold the volume buttons, or a key combination on a Bluetooth/USB keyboard (an optional accessibility service, which only checks key presses, extends them to other apps)</li>
<li>Rolling pre-trigger buffer, so the first word is never cut off</li>
<li>Continuous playback through the list you are viewing, with short beginning and ending chimes</li>
<li>Built-in, USB and Bluetooth microphones</li>
<li>WAV files stored locally; search, favorites, playback speed and ZIP export</li>
</ul>
<p>Voice Memo was previously called "Ciao Linda" and uses a new application ID. It is installed as a separate app and cannot access data of the old app.</p>
<p>Optional transcription (off by default) uses the speech recognition service provided by your device, which may be proprietary or use the network.</p>
