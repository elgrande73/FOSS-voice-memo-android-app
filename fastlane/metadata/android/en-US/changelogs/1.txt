First release as Voice Memo (formerly Ciao Linda, new application ID).
- Automatic playback of the next memo in the displayed list, with beginning and ending chimes
- Recording start/stop chimes
- Single "Match confidence threshold" setting (20–80 %) shown on the dashboard as "Trigger threshold"
- Recorded wake phrase, test recording and test result are kept across restarts and updates
- Lock-screen controls, volume-button and keyboard shortcuts
- New launcher icon
